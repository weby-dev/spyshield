# How to install SpyShield on your Android phone

A simple, step-by-step guide. No computer and no technical knowledge needed. It takes about two minutes.

> **You need:** an Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer. SpyShield does not work on iPhone.
>
> **Not sure of your Android version?** Open **Settings → About phone** and look for **Android version**.

---

## Step 1: Download the app

**Do this on your phone**, not on a computer.

1. Open this link in your phone's browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, or any other):

   **[Download SpyShield.apk](https://github.com/OWNER_PLACEHOLDER/spyshield/releases/latest/download/SpyShield.apk)**

2. Your browser may show a warning such as *"File might be harmful"* or *"Download anyway?"*.
   Tap **Download** / **Download anyway**.
   *(Every app downloaded from outside the Play Store gets this warning. It doesn't mean SpyShield is harmful.)*

3. Wait a few seconds for the download to finish.

---

## Step 2: Open the file

You'll see a **"Download complete"** message. Tap **Open**.

**Missed it?** Open your **Files** app (it may be called *Files by Google*, *My Files* or *File Manager*), go to **Downloads**, and tap **SpyShield.apk**.

---

## Step 3: Allow your phone to install it

The first time, Android may show:

> *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*

1. Tap **Settings** on that message.
2. Switch on **Allow from this source** (on some phones it says **Install unknown apps** or **Allow app installs**).
3. Press the **back** button. You'll return to the install screen.

This permission only applies to the app you downloaded with (your browser or Files app). You can switch it back off after installing.

---

## Step 4: Install

Tap **Install** and wait a few seconds.

### If Google Play Protect shows a warning

You might see *"Unsafe app blocked"*, *"App scan recommended"* or *"Play Protect doesn't recognise this app"*.

1. Tap **More details** (or the small arrow).
2. Tap **Install anyway**.

If it offers to **Scan app**, you can do that too; it's harmless. Play Protect shows this for apps it hasn't seen in the Play Store. SpyShield's full source code is public on GitHub for anyone to check.

When it says **App installed**, tap **Open**.

---

## Step 5: Set up SpyShield

SpyShield guides you through this the first time you open it.

### 1. Turn on Real-time protection
Flip the **Real-time protection** switch on the main screen.

### 2. Allow Usage Access
This lets SpyShield **name the app** using your camera or microphone.

1. Tap **Grant access**.
2. Find **SpyShield** in the list and tap it.
3. Switch on **Permit usage access** (or **Allow usage tracking**).
4. Press **back** to return to SpyShield.

The information never leaves your phone.

### 3. Allow notifications
Tap **Allow** when asked. Without notifications SpyShield can't warn you.

### 4. Let it run in the background (very important)
Many phones close background apps to save battery. If that happens to SpyShield, protection stops **without telling you**.

First tap **Allow** when SpyShield asks to stop optimising its battery use. Then check your phone maker's setting:

| Your phone | What to do |
|---|---|
| **OPPO / Realme** | Settings → Apps → App management → **SpyShield** → Battery usage → turn on **Allow background activity**. Also turn on **Allow auto launch** if you see it. |
| **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO** | Settings → Apps → Manage apps → **SpyShield** → Battery saver → **No restrictions**. Also turn on **Autostart**. |
| **Samsung** | Settings → Apps → **SpyShield** → Battery → **Unrestricted**. |
| **Vivo / iQOO** | Settings → Battery → Background power consumption management → **SpyShield** → **Allow high background power consumption**. Also allow **Auto-start** if your phone has it. |
| **Google Pixel and most others** | Settings → Apps → **SpyShield** → App battery usage → **Unrestricted**. |

**Tip:** Menu names change a little between models. If you can't find it, open **Settings** and search for **battery** or **background**.

### 5. Optional extras
- **Privacy dot:** shows a small dot on top of any app while your camera or mic is on. Turn it on in SpyShield and allow **Display over other apps** when asked.
- **Auto-block:** when an app you aren't using turns on your camera or mic and SpyShield is sure which one it is, it adds that app to your block list and sounds an alarm with a **Stop it now** button. Tap it, then tap **Force stop** on the screen that opens. (Android does not let SpyShield close other apps by itself, so this last tap is yours.) Auto-block is off until you turn it on.
- **Quick Settings tile:** swipe down twice from the top of the screen, tap the **pencil / edit** icon, and drag **SpyShield** into your tiles.
- **Home-screen widget:** long-press an empty spot on your home screen → **Widgets** → **SpyShield**.

**You're protected.** SpyShield now runs quietly in the background and warns you when something uses your camera or microphone.

## Updating to a new version

1. Download the latest file from the same link: **[SpyShield.apk](https://github.com/OWNER_PLACEHOLDER/spyshield/releases/latest/download/SpyShield.apk)**
2. Open it and tap **Update** (or **Install**).

**Don't uninstall the old version first.** Installing on top keeps your settings, block list and history.

---

## Something not working?

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| *"App not installed"* | You may have an older copy from a different source. Uninstall SpyShield, then install again. Also check you have some free storage. |
| *"There was a problem parsing the package"* | The download didn't finish, or your Android is older than 8.0. Download the file again. |
| No alerts at all | Check Real-time protection is on, notifications are allowed, and the battery setting in Step 5.4 is done. Then restart your phone. |
| It always says **Unknown app** | Usage Access is probably off. Turn it on (Step 5.2). |
| Alerts during face unlock or calls | Tap **Not a spy** on the alert, or mark the app as **Trusted**. |
